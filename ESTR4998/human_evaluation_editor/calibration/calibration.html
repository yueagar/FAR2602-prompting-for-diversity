How to fill this in

Round 1 stays as recorded. Nothing here changes either rater's round-1 sheet or its result. This sheet records your own explanation; it is not a new rating and not a reliability result.

Fill it in on your own first. For each pair, write what the central content of each response is and whether it is the same, before comparing with the other rater. Instrument outputs are not shown.

Written definition: two responses are equivalent if a person who had read one would gain essentially nothing from reading the other: the same central plot, character, idea, or image, differing only in wording, names, formatting, length, ordering, or minor detail. They are not equivalent if the central content differs, even when the structure or tone is shared.

Candidate clarification, to discuss: judge only the central content: what happens, who it happens to, the main idea or image. Shared writing style, tone, genre, setting type or story structure does not make two responses equivalent. Ask: after reading A, would reading B tell me a different story or idea?
