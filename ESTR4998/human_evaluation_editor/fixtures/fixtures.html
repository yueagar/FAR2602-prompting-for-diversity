How to check

What this is. These are the constructed test cases with known answers that every candidate judge will be scored on. A judge can only be shown to work if the cases themselves are right, so check that each one is what its label says. You are not rating quality or diversity.

Work on your own. Do not look at the other member's sheet or discuss items until you have both finished. Items marked cross-check or both members are also checked by the other member, and the disagreements are counted as the check's own reliability.

Outline items: answer yes only if every outline suits the task and they clearly differ in central content: the events and ending of a story, the central element of a description, a character's motivation and backstory, the central turn of a life. Different names, settings, viewpoints or wording alone do not count. A set is accepted only if both members say yes.

Response items: read the outline first, then the response. A variant (duplicate, paraphrase, names changed, padding) must also keep its source's central content; padding must add nothing useful to the task.

Pair items: A is the original and B the changed copy. Check that the change is exactly what the category says. For the strict categories, also check the difference is large enough to deserve a different score out of 10; otherwise answer no so the pair is rebuilt.

Answers: yes, no or unsure. For every no or unsure, write one sentence in the note saying why. A failed response is replaced by a new one of the same outline and variant type, never relabelled.
