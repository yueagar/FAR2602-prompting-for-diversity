How to inspect

Work on your own. Do not look at the other member's answers, or discuss items, until you have both finished. The inspection never changes an instrument: write problems down and fix them later as a reviewed change.

Read the texts first. Form your own view, then press Show the instrument's verdict to see the score, which end of the ranking the item comes from, and any per-response labels. Then record whether the score matches what you see.

Order. Items follow the guide's reading order: the within-prompt sets first, then single responses, then the overall extremes. If you run out of time, stop and note where.

For every highest-scoring item, also check for reward hacking: irrelevance; padding or verbosity; the same story with new names, settings or adjectives; lists of alternatives instead of one answer; instruction leakage; disclaimers or refusals; degenerate text (non-English fragments, loops, cut off at the token ceiling).

Your answers: yes if the score matches what you see, no for a mismatch, unsure otherwise, with one sentence in the note.
